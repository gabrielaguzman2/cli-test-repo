I am Gabriela Guzman, a postdoc at CIMAT Mexico. My math interests are in algebraic geometry, motivic homotopy theory, K-theory, enumerative geometry, and log geometry.


For the moment, I have been using LLMs (Gemini) mainly as search engines and as assistance to read/understand papers.

For a project, I would like to get more involved in building "something" which allows me to learn more computer science techniques in the process.

So I find Candace's idea very appealing—not only as a companion tool for research papers, but also as some kind of teaching assistant.


Thinking about research, the kind of problems I would like to tackle using LLMs are around **motivic log relative enumerative problems**; more specifically, in many counting problems, a strong hypothesis is that our geometric object is smooth.
A1-homotopy theory has been applied to enrich enumerative problems to arbitrary fields. On the other hand, motivic constructions have been performed in the setting of logarithmic geometry, where mild singularities are aloud,  but few concrete computations have been performed in this setting. So a problem I would like to try is to extend the motivic ideas to the log setting.
